A keyword gap report is one of the easiest things in SEO to produce and one of the easiest to misuse. Export the difference between two sites and you get several thousand rows, most of which are noise. The tool above produces the difference; the work is in the filtering, and that is what the rest of this page is about.

## Choose the right comparison set

The instinct is to use the client's named competitors. That is often wrong. The sites you compete with in search are whoever occupies the results for your target queries, and that set regularly includes publishers, review sites, and marketplaces that nobody at the client would describe as a competitor.

Run a few of your priority queries and note who actually appears. That list is your comparison set. Using the commercial competitor list instead produces gaps against companies that may not rank at all, which is a report about nothing.

## Strip out the gaps that are not gaps

Before anything gets planned, remove:

- **Their branded queries.** You will never rank for a competitor's brand name, and you should not want to.
- **Careers, location, and support queries.** These are gaps in the literal sense and irrelevant in every other sense.
- **Business-model mismatches.** If the competitor ranks for queries served by a marketplace or a free tool your client does not have, the content will not satisfy the intent.
- **Anything on a fortress SERP.** If the competitor holds the position because of a decade of accumulated authority, the gap is a description of the authority difference, not an opportunity.

That last filter usually removes more rows than the other three combined, and it is the one most often skipped. Check the SERP before you plan the page, not after you have written it.

## What a usable output looks like

After filtering, each surviving row should carry enough to make a decision:

`Keyword | Which competitor ranks | Their position | Intent | Beatable? | Cluster | Page type`

The `Beatable?` column is the one that turns a report into a plan. It is a judgement, made by looking at the SERP, and writing it down forces the judgement to happen before a writer is assigned rather than after a page fails.

## Cluster before you brief

A filtered gap list is still a list of keywords, and briefing keywords one at a time is how sites end up cannibalising themselves. Group the survivors by intent, so that keywords answerable by a single page become a single page. The [keyword clustering method](/tools/keyword-cluster-mapper) covers how to do that grouping and how to assign page types to the result.

## Changes under validation

- Extract the approved article's explicit promises from its headline and effective instructions before the model sees research questions or publisher context. A second call maps those immutable promises to the questions. Every promise carries its exact headline/instruction span, expected useful answer and question mapping. One bounded question repair may fill a gap; it cannot narrow the headline or change the buyer. A general task may use a named product example without acquiring an unpromised requirement to explain that product.
- Use fixed question keys in the provider response and derive numeric references on the server. Missing, invented or optional references do not become approval. Source readiness separately checks both questions and promises.
- Require a versioned sentence inventory in factual review. Each sentence needs its claims or an explicit nonfactual decision. Both reviewers inspect the same normalized draft; an exact-title replacement or later duplicate cleanup cannot invalidate the claimed coverage silently. Sentence-weighted batches retain the existing eight-call, three-concurrent, 90-second limit.
- Carry standing workspace article instructions through preparation, the cache fingerprint, choice rechecks, first-month work and generation. Changed instructions invalidate the prepared packet before writing and quota consumption; failed settings reads do not become empty instructions.
- Preserve measured keyword records when choosing a final editorial task, use verified product-evidence references, and pass the requested language/market into discovery instead of inheriting an English homepage's language. Opportunity policy version 9 and preparation version 2 invalidate older contracts.
- Ask the writer for a supported concrete application, essential operating explanations and a natural ending. Preserve a closing link already present instead of appending a duplicate call to action. Add a visible jump from the full draft preview to its trial plan.
- Distinguish fictional example inputs and team-defined completion conventions from factual product behavior. Examples must preserve the product's tracking unit, states and configured triggers; separate capabilities do not establish an automatic workflow. The same general context rule applies to initial and recovery claim review.
- Convert the draft into the editor document before review, review its rendered HTML, and save that same document. Preserve mixed inline/block content in lists and table cells, quoted attributes and decoded entities. Reject any post-review change to the reviewed HTML before consuming allowance.
- Reconcile numerical checks only against complete current sentence coverage, preserving explicit contradictions and factual or mixed sentences. Keep exempted illustrative numbers visible as reviewed nonfactual inputs. Reuse on manual or automatic approval requires an exact saved-HTML hash; edited or legacy coverage must be reviewed again.

## Integrated verification

The final integrated unit run passed **3,692 tests across 347 files**, with 70 tests in two files skipped. The production build, including TypeScript, passed. Lint passed across all 63 changed/new TypeScript files, with one existing unused-variable warning in the publish action. Workspace-scope regressions are part of the full unit suite.

The gate-enabled browser lane passed all three tests with zero retries during this implementation, including expired-source and withheld-draft recovery, trial activation and duplicate delivery, quota, first-month continuation, the preserved first draft, and the preview-to-plan jump. The screenshots use synthetic article/provider/Stripe fixtures; they do not establish generated article quality. This run preceded the final review corrections. Frozen-revision CI and the ten-case cohort are still pending.

Independent final review found and corrected two additional boundary defects: nonbreaking-space title normalization could reject unchanged content after serialization, and matching numerical checks by display-truncated sentence text could both hold an innocent long sentence and exempt the wrong factual sentence. Title comparison now preserves equivalent whitespace without rewriting accepted HTML. Numerical reconciliation now requires lossless full sentence text and current detector offsets; truncation is display-only. Regression tests and the original independent probes verify both corrections.

## Release boundary

The baseline does not meet the required first-impression quality. Current implementation checks, new writer output, the full gate flow and a fresh cohort must be recorded before any release-readiness claim. The prospective next cohort retains all eight earlier cases and adds Buffer and Osprey as two new holdouts; previously inspected outputs are regression cases. Predeclared feasibility labels are hypotheses, not human gold labels.

Raw source packets, provider responses, candidates and receipts remain outside Git in the protected `onboarding-evidence-validation` directory. Earlier failures are not replaced by successful retries. Production migrations, merge, deployment and post-deployment verification remain separate from this local work.

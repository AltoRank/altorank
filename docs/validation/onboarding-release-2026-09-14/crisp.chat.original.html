How to choose a conversational AI platform: pricing, training, and channel coverage compared

A conversational AI platform is software that lets a business build, train, and deploy AI agents that answer customers over chat, voice, or messaging apps instead of relying only on human agents typing every reply. Picking one comes down to three practical questions: how the platform prices automation, how you train its AI agent on your product knowledge, and which messaging channels it actually supports out of the box. This guide compares Crisp, Retell AI, and Fini AI against those three criteria, then walks through a worked scenario for a small support team choosing between them.

How does AI agent training work on each platform?

Training method affects how fast you get a usable agent live and how much ongoing maintenance it needs.

  • Crisp: the platform automatically browses your website for you, and you can also import PDFs and write knowledge base articles to teach the AI agent about your product, according to Crisp's own product page.
  • Retell AI: its Knowledge Base feature lets you import single webpages or an entire site, upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT documents, or add custom text directly, with retrieval Retell describes as running "under 100ms."
  • Fini AI: onboarding connects your existing helpdesk (Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce, and others) through one-click OAuth, then moves through a "train your agent" and "test with AI" step before going live; Fini also markets a self-updating "Knowledge Atlas" that detects its own knowledge gaps over time, a claim made on Fini's own site rather than independently verified here.

If your team already keeps documentation in a public help center, Crisp's website crawl and Retell AI's URL import both shortcut the setup work. If your knowledge lives inside an existing helpdesk, Fini AI's OAuth connection avoids re-uploading it elsewhere.

What onboarding and support should you expect at each pricing tier?

Support level typically scales with plan tier across all three platforms, so check what's included before you assume "enterprise-grade support" comes standard.

Crisp offers a 14-day free trial with full access to all features and no card required on every self-serve plan, while its Enterprise tier adds dedicated onboarding, a personalized SLA, and team training, according to the Crisp pricing page. Retell AI's pay-as-you-go tier includes community and email support, and its Enterprise plan adds dedicated implementation support as an add-on plus 24/7 omnichannel support through a dedicated portal. Fini AI structures onboarding by plan: Growth includes implementation and shared customer success, Scale adds white-glove onboarding and a dedicated AI engineer, and Enterprise adds a 90-day free pilot with a dedicated engineer pod and a 15-minute SLA, per Fini's pricing page. Fini also states agents can go live in 14 days and reach full autonomy in 30, a timeline Fini publishes itself rather than one confirmed by outside testing.

A worked example: matching a support team's channel mix to a platform

Here is a hypothetical scenario to make the tradeoffs concrete. Picture a six-person support team at a small e-commerce store handling roughly 600 conversations a month across a website chat widget, WhatsApp, and email, with occasional questions arriving through Instagram DMs.

Channel fit rules Retell AI out first: its native strength is voice and phone, and the team in this scenario has no meaningful call volume, so a per-minute voice platform would be paying for infrastructure it barely uses. Between Crisp and Fini AI, channel coverage again matters: Crisp's plan comparison lists WhatsApp Business, Instagram, and email as native channels at the Essentials tier ($95/month, 10 seats included), while Fini AI's channel row covers chat, email, and widget but does not list Instagram or WhatsApp specifically in its own pricing table.

Pricing predictability is the next filter. At roughly 600 conversations a month, Crisp's flat $95/month Essentials plan gives a fixed number to budget against, while Fini AI's per-resolution model (starting at $0.89 per resolution on the Growth plan) charges only for tickets the AI actually resolves, which can suit a team expecting spiky volume but makes the monthly bill harder to predict at low volume. For this specific brief, the team should trial Crisp's Essentials plan first given the channel match and flat pricing, and revisit Fini AI if ticket volume grows past a few thousand a month and the team wants a resolution-based guarantee instead.

What are the best conversational AI platforms?

There is no single best conversational AI platform for every support team; the right one depends on which channel carries most of your volume and how you want to be billed. Crisp fits teams centralizing web chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, and email into one shared inbox under flat per-workspace pricing. Retell AI fits teams whose support or sales volume runs mostly through phone calls and want usage-based billing tied to call minutes. Fini AI fits teams in regulated or high-ticket-volume environments that want a per-resolution price and a helpdesk-integrated setup. For a broader list beyond these three, Retell AI's own roundup of conversational AI platforms profiles additional voice- and enterprise-focused options worth checking against the same three criteria: pricing model, native channels, and training method.

Start by listing your own channel mix and monthly volume, then run that list against each platform's own pricing and channel pages rather than a general ranking.
